Legal company versus seller

Compare the Chinese registered company with the seller named on the quote, PI, or contract. A real license does not prove that the same entity is responsible for your order.

Payee versus transaction

Check whether the bank beneficiary belongs to the seller. If a Hong Kong company or export agent receives payment, its role should be explained and documented before funds move.

Factory claim versus evidence

Connect the claimed factory, operating address, workshop evidence, relevant process, and the product you are buying. A factory video without that connection proves very little.

Decision and missing evidence

See what aligns, what remains unverified, and what must be answered before payment. The recommendation is to proceed, pause for evidence, compare further, or step back.

What we actually review

A useful verification starts with real supplier materials, not only a company name.

The strongest first-pass checks usually combine a supplier link, product context, quote logic, and at least one piece of visual or document proof. China Partner Hub can work from partial information, but stronger inputs produce a sharper go / no-go recommendation.

Company identity chain

Alibaba shop, website, Chinese business license, registered address, contact details, and the company named on commercial documents should describe one understandable transaction.

PI and payment chain

The quotation or PI, seller name, bank beneficiary, account location, payment terms, and any third-party exporter should fit together before you approve a deposit.

Production responsibility

Ask which entity owns or controls the site, which steps are in-house, what is subcontracted, who made the sample, and who is expected to make the bulk order.

Product evidence

Samples, specifications, certificate holder names, product models, workshop evidence, packaging, and quote logic must relate to the exact product and order under review.

Factory context

The workshop environment should support the supplier story, not contradict it.

Print, stitching, and packing details often reveal more than a polished storefront page.

Sensitive files do not need to be public. You can send catalogs, quotes, screenshots, or short clips by email or WhatsApp after payment.
